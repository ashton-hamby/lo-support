APPLICATIONS

Starting a New Application

Submit applications through the approved loan origination system. Include context that may not be obvious from the application itself.

  • Borrower's goals and timeline
  • Purchase or refinance details
  • Property and occupancy considerations
  • Known income, credit, or asset concerns
  • Any special lender or product preference
HANDOFF

Application Notes Template

Use this quick structure when sending an application for review:

  • Borrower and purpose: who, what, and why
  • Timeline: target pre-approval or closing date
  • Loan priorities: payment, cash to close, product
  • Known concerns: income, assets, credit, property
  • Action needed: questions or next steps

Enter borrower-specific details only in approved secure systems.

PROCESS

Four-Step Support Process

1. Intake: Review application and identify missing items.

2. Validate: Review qualifying information, AUS, and loan structure.

3. Deliver: Prepare qualification or pre-approval support for LO review.

4. Transition: Organize disclosures, file narrative, and processing handoff.

RESPONSIBILITIES

Who Handles What?

LO: Lead and initial sales conversation; borrower and agent relationships; loan options, rates, payments, structure and financial advice; pricing confirmation; weekly borrower and agent status calls; final CD and cash-to-close discussion.

LO Support: Post-application qualification; document needs; AUS and guideline analysis; pre-approval issuance; pricing scenarios for LO review; disclosures preparation; lock requests after LO confirmation; submission; processor coordination; troubleshooting; twice-weekly pipeline reports; closing oversight.

File-specific responsibilities are confirmed during onboarding.

How we work together

AFTER APPLICATION

Qualification and Pre-Approval

I review the application, introduce myself to the borrower, request documents, and work through qualification. I can independently finalize and issue the pre-approval. You notify the borrower, deliver the initial letter, and discuss next steps. I handle updated and property-specific letters.

UNDER CONTRACT

Disclosures and Submission

I review the contract, update the file, request missing items, rerun AUS as needed, and prepare pricing and structure scenarios. You discuss the options with the borrower and confirm their decision. I prepare the file for disclosures, monitor signatures, and submit the loan to processing.

IN PROCESSING

Issues and Escalations

I serve as the processor’s primary LO contact, research issues, and identify potential solutions. If borrower-facing financial decisions or material concerns arise, I bring you the issue, facts, and options so you can advise the borrower.

THROUGH CLOSING

Visibility and Closing

I send pipeline reports twice each week and escalate urgent matters directly. I monitor closing activity and review the final CD internally. You review final figures and cash to close with the borrower, communicate closing logistics, and maintain the relationship through closing.

Frequently asked questions

When do you become involved?

Once the borrower submits a loan application. You own the lead, initial sales conversation, and relationship before application.

Who talks with borrowers?

I handle routine operational communication and documentation needs. You handle pricing, rates, payments, structure, financial decisions, material qualification changes, and significant concerns.

Who requests rate locks?

You discuss pricing and confirm the borrower’s decision. Once you communicate that decision to me, I submit the lock request.

How often will I receive pipeline updates?

Twice each week, with direct escalation when your attention is needed before the next report.

What loan types do you support?

Experience includes Conventional, FHA, VA, USDA, Non-QM, HELOCs, purchases, and refinances.

What are your working hours?

Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM Central Time. After-hours help may be available for genuinely time-sensitive matters but is not guaranteed.

Can I customize the process?

Reasonable communication and workflow preferences can be accommodated. The core ownership structure and priority system stay standardized.

What if my borrower is writing an offer?

Flag the genuine urgency so I can evaluate and reprioritize appropriately.
