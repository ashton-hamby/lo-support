New LO Onboarding

From the LO's decision to work together through the first applications.

When to begin

The LO has confirmed they are ready to work together. Compensation has already been established; it is not part of this onboarding process.

Onboarding is complete when Primary LOA access in Arive and CIC soft- and hard-pull credentials are verified. No confirmation email or onboarding call is required. Schedule a call only if needed.

1. Send the welcome email

Send the LO the LO Resource Center and these three actions:

  1. Review the Resource Center, including responsibilities and the workflow.
  2. Add Ashton as Primary LOA in Arive.
  3. Email CIC credentials for both soft and hard credit pulls to Ashton using the approved secure company email.

Use the copyable email below. No additional onboarding meeting is necessary by default.

2. Verify setup and resolve exceptions

  1. Confirm the LO has assigned Ashton as Primary LOA in Arive and the needed access is working.
  2. Confirm both CIC soft-pull and hard-pull access work.
  3. Follow up only on missing setup items or access issues.

Use the established standard credit-pull process and default lender/pricing workflow. Do not add unnecessary preference questionnaires or configuration steps.

3. Begin work

  1. New applications: Begin the Initial Application Review SOP as applications arrive.
  2. Existing pipeline: The LO identifies files requiring assistance by tagging Ashton in an Arive note describing what needs attention. Review and take ownership of those assigned items; do not audit every existing file by default.
  3. Borrower contact: Contact borrowers only when actively working on their file and borrower involvement is needed. Follow the established introduction process for new applications.
  4. Pipeline updates: Send twice-weekly pipeline updates only for loans in processing, when the LO has applicable loans. Do not include qualification-stage applications or pre-approvals.
