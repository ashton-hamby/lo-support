Initial Application Review

Follow the actual Arive workflow from initial qualification status through customized Client Needs, borrower outreach, and the final status note.

Review checklist

011. Move to Qualification⌄

In Arive, change the loan status to Qualification. This notifies the Loan Team that application review has begun.

022. Review Borrower Info⌄

Personal Info: completeness and citizenship or residency. Address: 24-month residential history and homeownership. Military Info: potential VA eligibility. Declarations: occupancy and any Yes responses requiring clarification.

033. Review Financial Info⌄

Monthly Income: employment and income sources, 24-month history, and gaps. Assets: accounts and gift funds. Monthly Liabilities: order soft credit pull and compare liabilities. Real Estate Owned: property use, ownership, and whether each property will be sold or retained.

044. Record Initial Concerns⌄

Identify missing application details, discrepancies, and possible qualification issues to clarify.

055. Review Credit⌄

Review all reported scores, remove duplicate liabilities in Arive, confirm payments, review collections, charge-offs, medical collections, public records, and recent inquiries. Ask whether inquiries resulted in new credit. Apply collection-payment conventions only where permitted by the applicable program and lender.

066A. Universal Needs⌄

For each borrower request photo ID and the Opt Out of Spam Calls information.

076B. Residency⌄

For permanent residents request the green card. For non-permanent residents request the applicable visa and/or EAD. No extra residency request for U.S. citizens.

086C. Military / VA COE⌄

For current or former military service, attempt to obtain the Certificate of Eligibility electronically. Save it if obtained; otherwise request the COE from the borrower.

096D. Declarations⌄

For every Yes declaration other than intended occupancy, request appropriate clarification or supporting documentation.

106E. Income⌄

W-2: most recent 30 days of pay stubs, two years of W-2s, and two years of final year-end pay stubs. Self-employed: two years of personal federal returns and, if not Schedule C, two years of federal business returns. For other income sources, select the corresponding Client Needs Library request and apply program-specific requirements.

116F. Assets and Gifts⌄

Request the two most recent statements for each applicable asset account listed on the application. If gift funds are indicated, request gift details.

126G. Liabilities⌄

For each mortgage reporting on credit, request the most recent mortgage statement.

136H. Real Estate Owned⌄

Reconcile REO with mortgages on credit; request clarification for discrepancies. Request current homeowners insurance declarations pages for every property, HOA dues statements where applicable, and two years of personal federal tax returns for rental income when investment properties are owned and returns were not already requested for self-employment.

146I. Final Client Needs Review⌄

Check the entire list for completeness, unnecessary duplicates, and assignment of each request to the correct borrower.

157. Email Client Needs⌄

Email the finalized customized Client Needs list to the borrower from Arive and confirm it was sent.

168. Send Introduction Text⌄

Send the existing introduction text to all borrowers, personalized for borrower names, LO name, and current company branding.

179. Add Arive Status Note⌄

Record loan purpose, estimated loan amount, each borrower’s reported credit scores, application concerns, Client Needs status, next steps, and any LO action needed.

Borrower introduction text

Process boundary

This SOP covers the initial application review and document requests. Detailed income and asset analysis, AUS, loan structuring, and qualification decisions occur after supporting documentation is received. Follow current agency, investor, and lender requirements.
