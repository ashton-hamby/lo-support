Borrower Document Follow-Up

Follow up on outstanding Client Needs, honor borrower-provided upload deadlines, and route stalled document collection back to the Loan Officer for re-engagement.

Daily file review and Arive Status Note

Review the file each day while borrower documents remain outstanding, including when the borrower has provided an expected upload date.

  1. Check whether any documents have been uploaded since the previous review.
  2. Record whether borrower follow-up was sent that day, or explain why it was not sent (for example, an agreed upload deadline has not passed).
  3. Record whether the borrower is responding to communications, including any promised upload date or relevant update.
  4. Leave a dated Arive Status Note summarizing the document activity, communication, and next step.

A daily review does not require contacting the borrower every day. Continue to honor borrower-provided upload deadlines and the 48-hour escalation rule below.

01

Follow up on outstanding documents

After the customized Client Needs are sent, continue following up with the borrower for documents that have not been uploaded.

02

Check for a borrower-provided deadline

If the borrower provides a specific date or deadline when they will upload documents, wait until that deadline passes before following up again.

If documents are still missing after the deadline, resume borrower follow-up.

03

Apply the 48-hour escalation rule

If no upload deadline has been provided and at least 48 hours have passed without a document upload, create an Arive task assigned to the Loan Officer to re-engage the borrower.

The trigger is the absence of a document upload for at least 48 hours with no borrower-provided upload deadline. Do not substitute a response alone for a document upload.

Decision guide

Borrower gave an upload deadline? Wait until it passes; if documents are still outstanding, follow up again.

No deadline and 48+ hours without uploaded documents? Create the LO re-engagement task in Arive.

Documents uploaded within 48 hours? Continue the normal document collection process for any remaining needs.
