What I do

Built to help you produce more.

This is more than administrative support. I analyze, research, prepare, issue, submit, coordinate, troubleshoot, and monitor the work behind your loans. You remain focused on the relationships and conversations that require you as the Loan Officer.

01 / INTAKE

Application Review

Review new applications, identify missing information, organize borrower needs, and prepare the file for qualification.

02 / VALIDATE

Qualification & Structure

Review income, assets and credit; run AUS; evaluate loan options and prepare an informed recommendation for the LO.

03 / DELIVER

Pre-Approval Support

Complete technical qualification and independently finalize and issue pre-approvals. The LO delivers the initial letter and discusses next steps; I handle subsequent updated and property-specific letters.

04 / TRANSITION

Processing Handoff

Prepare the file, disclosure instructions, and lock details for Local Processing, which executes disclosures and lender lock requests on our behalf.

05 / FOLLOW THROUGH

Pipeline Coordination

Serve as Local Processing’s primary LO contact, troubleshoot conditions, oversee closing activity and final CD review, and provide pipeline reporting twice each week.

06 / PARTNERSHIP

Clear Division of Roles

You own borrower and referral relationships, pricing and financial advice, weekly status updates, and business development. I provide the analysis and operational execution.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Will I still manage my borrower and referral partner relationships?

Yes. You remain responsible for your client and referral relationships. My role is to support the technical and operational side of the file.

Do you help with pre-approvals?

Yes. Application review, income and asset analysis, AUS, loan structure, and pre-approval preparation are core parts of my support model.

How do we get started?

We begin with a conversation about your production, workflow, expectations, and whether the support model is a good fit. Then we establish credit preferences, approved access, and application handoffs. See the Getting Started guide.

Can I use my preferred lender?

We can discuss lender preferences and file-specific needs as part of our workflow. Final lender selection and pricing decisions are coordinated with the LO.

How much does support cost?

Compensation details are discussed directly based on the applicable agreement and company requirements.
