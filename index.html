Meet your Loan Partner

Hi, I'm Ashton.

I've been a licensed Loan Officer since 2019, so I understand the work on both sides of the partnership: the borrower conversations that drive production and the detailed analysis and execution needed to get loans across the finish line.

Today, I focus on being the Loan Partner behind the scenes. I support multiple Loan Officers with a consistent process, a thorough approach to qualification, and the willingness to dig into complicated scenarios instead of simply passing problems along.

My goal is straightforward: give you the confidence that your files are being handled so you have more time to do the work that grows your business.

The partnership

I own the execution. You own the relationships.

You don't have to change how you build relationships or run your business. You remain the primary advisor and relationship owner. I step in after the application to handle the detailed qualification and operational work, keeping you informed and bringing you in when a decision needs your attention.

What I own

Technical analysis, operational preparation, coordination, and follow-through.

  • Reviewing applications and documentation
  • Calculating income and analyzing credit, assets, liabilities, and eligibility
  • Running AUS and researching guidelines
  • Evaluating programs, pricing, and loan structure scenarios
  • Preparing and issuing pre-approvals
  • Preparing files for disclosures and processing
  • Troubleshooting issues, supporting processing, and monitoring through closing

What you own

The relationships and decisions that grow your business.

  • Prospecting and converting leads
  • Building and maintaining referral relationships
  • Advising borrowers on loan options, pricing, payments, and financial decisions
  • Confirming borrower decisions before lock instructions are coordinated
  • Communicating proactively with borrowers and referral partners
  • Growing your production
Your relationships stay yours.Keep your sales conversations, referral partnerships, and borrower-facing decisions. I work behind the scenes within a consistent process, so delegating file execution doesn't mean losing control or visibility.
More than task support

You need answers, not just updates.

I don't simply flag a problem and hand it back to you. I work through the details, identify why something isn't working, research the guidelines, and explore potential solutions. Then I bring you the information you need to make the right borrower-facing recommendation.

Qualification & analysis

Income, assets, credit, liabilities, real estate owned, AUS findings, and documentation.

Guidelines & loan structure

Program eligibility, lender requirements, pricing scenarios, and alternative paths forward.

Pre-approvals

Technical qualification, initial pre-approval issuance, and updated or property-specific letters.

Contract & submission

Contract review, file preparation, disclosure instructions, and coordinated processing handoff.

Processing support

Primary LO contact for processing, condition troubleshooting, and qualification issues.

Through closing

Pipeline monitoring, closing oversight, and final Closing Disclosure review.
