Income & Employment

Pay Stubs

Please upload your two most recent paystubs that cover at least the last 30 days so we can confirm your current income. The paystubs must be dated within the past 30 days to be accepted for approval. Be sure to print or download the documents directly from your employer or payroll website, since we can review only official copies. We are not able to use screenshots or phone captures in place of the actual paystubs.

Income & Employment

Year End Pay Stubs

Please upload the final pay stub you received in December for each of the last two calendar years so we can confirm your full earnings, including bonuses, commissions, or any income beyond your base pay. Make sure each pay stub shows the year-to-date total that matches that same year and not the start of the next year.

Income & Employment

Latest W-2's

Please upload all W-2 forms from the most recent two years so we can verify your income for the approval. We can accept only W-2s dated within the last two years. If you worked for more than one employer during that period, include every W-2 you received from each employer so your file reflects the full history correctly.

Assets

Bank Statements

Please upload your official bank statements for the most recent two months so we can verify the funds available for your down payment and closing costs. The statements must include all pages, even if some pages are blank, and they need to be dated within the last 60 days to be accepted for approval. Be sure to print or download the statements directly from your bank or online banking website, since we can review only official copies and we are not able to use screenshots or phone captures in place of the actual statements.

Identification

Photo ID

Please upload a clear copy of your unexpired photo ID so we can confirm your legal name matches the application and the other documents we receive. You can provide any one of these forms of ID, such as a driver’s license, passport, state ID card, military ID, permanent resident card.
